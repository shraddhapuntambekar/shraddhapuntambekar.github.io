The Wonders Club is a little dream that Diti, my best friend, and I are bringing to life, inspired by our own experiences as mothers. We understand how challenging it can be for parents to find meaningful and enjoyable activities for their children every weekend, so we envisioned a place where that decision is already taken care of. Created for children aged 4–8, the club will offer two joyful hours every Saturday filled with friendship, stories, games, art, movement, hands-on activities and learning through play. Through The Wonders Club, we hope to create a warm and welcoming community where children feel a sense of belonging, freely explore their curiosity and creativity, grow in confidence and, most importantly, have something wonderful to look forward to each week.
