A weekend project

The Wonders Club

The Wonders Club is a little dream that Diti, my best friend, and I are bringing to life, inspired by our own experiences as mothers. Created for children aged 4–8, the club offers joyful weekend hours filled with friendship, stories, games, art, movement, hands-on activities and learning through play.

Through The Wonders Club, we hope to create a warm and welcoming community where children feel a sense of belonging, freely explore their curiosity and creativity, grow in confidence and, most importantly, have something wonderful to look forward to each week.
